I'm delighted to announce that I'm starting a new position as **AI Research Scientist** at **Hydro-Québec**! 🔬⚡

## Research Focus

At Hydro-Québec's research centers, I'll be focusing on:

- **Advanced Time Series Foundation Models**: Developing cutting-edge foundation models for multivariate time series on graphs, specialized in temporal, spatial, and geometric data analysis, relationship modeling, and prediction
- **Innovative Trading Strategies**: Leveraging machine learning to power sophisticated financial market strategies
- **Energy Sector Applications**: Translating AI/ML innovations to renewable energy optimization and management

## Organizations

I'm excited to work at **Institut de Recherche d'Hydro-Québec (IREQ)** in the Data Science and AI division, collaborating closely with **Le Parquet** (The Floor) — Hydro-Québec's energy trading floor in Montreal. This unique position bridges cutting-edge research with real-world trading operations, allowing me to translate advanced time series models and AI innovations directly into practical trading strategies for the energy market.

I'm thrilled to develop innovative AI solutions at the intersection of renewable energy, advanced machine learning, and financial technology! 🚀
