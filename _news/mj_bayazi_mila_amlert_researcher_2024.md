Excited to share that I've started my internship as an Applied Machine Learning Researcher at [Mila - Quebec Artificial Intelligence Institute (AMLERT)](https://mila.quebec/)!

I'm working on developing an LLM-based sequential recommender system, evaluating OpenAI GPT and open-source models, implementing retrieval-augmented generation, and instruct fine-tuning of LLMs—collaborating with an amazing and active team (Arsene Fansi Tchango and Jacob Lavoie). 

Looking forward to working on this cutting-edge project and contributing to the field of AI research!

**Internship Focus:**
- LLM-based Sequential Recommender System Development
- OpenAI GPT and Open-Source Model Evaluation
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Implementation
- Instruction Fine-Tuning of Large Language Models
- Collaborative Research with Mila Team
